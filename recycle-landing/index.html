Three phases, in order

How a launch works

1

Loss Mining

A window opens before each launch. The protocol measures how much PAPER every wallet minted on Papertrade. PAPER only comes from losing closes and liquidations, so it is a proof of loss anyone can verify on-chain. More PAPER means more tickets, on a square-root curve that caps whales.

PAPER snapshot · Merkle root on-chain
2

Bonding curve

Only ticket holders can buy. Each ticket unlocks a slice of buying power. Once PAPER is transferable, every buy burns PAPER; until then buyers pay in USDC and tickets are the filter. If a launch does not graduate before its deadline, buyers get a pro-rata refund.

PROOF OF LOSS · HYBRID · PAPER
3

Vault

At graduation half the raise goes to DEX liquidity and half to a vault that trades BTC or ETH under rules the creator picks at launch. Profits buy back and burn the meme. Losses mint PAPER for holders or for staking.

immutable rules · on-chain keeper

Phase 3 · the creator chooses

Three vaults, one choice at launch

The creator picks the mode when creating the meme, and the contract locks it forever. The keeper decides when to enter; the contract enforces leverage, allocation and where the money goes.

Multiple of the launch price after 90 days. Bar = 10th–90th percentile, dot = median.

Regenerative vault

One delta-neutral path: capital turns into staked PAPER, and the cycle yield climbs toward 100% of the starting capital.
